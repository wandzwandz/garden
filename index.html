Tomatoes

Whitefly: Tiny white insects under leaves. Action if heavy.

Aphids: Curling leaves, sticky residue.

Blight: Dark lesions during wet, warm weather.

Brassicas

Caterpillars: Large holes in leaves. Hand remove if low numbers.

Aphids: Colonies on leaf undersides.

Clubroot: Swollen roots, stunted growth.

Carrots

Carrot rust fly: Tunnels in roots. Use covers.

Aphids: Monitor young foliage.

Damping off: Seedlings collapse in wet soils.
