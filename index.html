Crop Guides (End-to-End)

Tomatoes: Plant after frost | Watch: aphids, caterpillars | Diseases: blight. Lifestyle block threshold: treat only if >15% plants affected.
Brassicas: Sow spring/autumn | Watch: white butterfly | Threshold: 1 caterpillar per 10 plants.
Carrots: Direct sow | Watch: carrot rust fly | Use rotation & covers before spraying.
Roses: Winter pruning | Watch: aphids | Treat only if buds distorted.

Pest & Disease ID Cards

Aphids
Action: treat only if new growth distorted.
Caterpillars
Action: lifestyle blocks – act at >10% leaf loss.
Powdery mildew
Action: improve airflow before spraying.
Blight
Action: remove infected plants immediately.

Lifestyle Block Rules of Thumb

Walk the block weekly and record changes.
Never spray without confirming thresholds.
One bad patch usually indicates drainage or fertility issues.
Rotate crops every season to reduce disease.
Hygiene prevents most pest and disease issues.
