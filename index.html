Crop Guides

Tomatoes

Planting: January - March (North), February - April (Central), March - May (South)

Pests: Aphids, Whitefly, Caterpillars

Diseases: Blight, Mildew

Lifestyle block IPM: Treat only if >10% leaf loss

Brassicas (Cabbage, Broccoli, Cauliflower)

Planting: February - May (North/Central), March - June (South)

Pests: White butterfly caterpillars, aphids

Diseases: Clubroot, Leaf spots

Lifestyle block IPM: Treat if >10% leaves damaged

Carrots

Planting: March - August (all regions)

Pests: Carrot rust fly, aphids

Diseases: Seedling damping off

Lifestyle block IPM: Observe damage before action

Pest & Disease ID Cards

Aphids

Small green insects, cluster on new growth

Action: >10% infestation, spray with soap

Whitefly

Small white insects, under leaves

Action: Use yellow sticky traps if heavy

Caterpillars

Chew leaves rapidly

Action: Remove by hand if few, otherwise safe insecticide
